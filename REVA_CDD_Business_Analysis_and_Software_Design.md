## 2. Course Description & Rationale

This course introduces students to business analysis techniques,
software requirement engineering, UML modelling, software design
principles, and agile development practices. Students learn to convert
business needs into well-structured software solutions using
industry-standard documentation and modelling techniques.

**Placement relevance:** Builds competencies expected in Business
Analyst, System Analyst, Software Engineer, Product Analyst, QA Analyst,
and Functional Consultant roles.
